This round's vinyl

Grooves are seeded by this round's telemetry, so every round presses a different record. It is a free SVG file: nothing is minted and no wallet is involved.

How it reads the wheel

  • Tempo follows the wheel: 40 + (rpm / 600)0.9 × 128 BPM, up to 168. A resting wheel plays at 40 BPM.
  • Mood comes from RPM. At 0 (or when the core reports IDLE) it is NAPPING: sparse bells and a blue glow. Below 500 it is JOGGING on A minor pentatonic. From 500 it is ZOOMIES on A major with a busier kick.
  • Melody is seeded from distanceMm and cumulativeWorkUnits at this browser's first reading of each round. The round keeps its tune as live tempo, drums and mood voicing change.
  • Bass root is blockHeight mod 8, a step on the scale, played on beats 1 and 3.
  • Drums put a kick on steps 0, 4, 8 and 12 and hats on the offbeats; more settlements in the last 24 hours fill in more hats.
  • Rest is respected: when Squeektoshi naps, the drums stop and the mixtape becomes a soft lullaby.
